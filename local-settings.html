In-page notifications

Use the bell button in the reader popup to turn all toast notifications on or off. When notifications are off, feeds continue refreshing and new articles are saved, but no toast notifications are queued. Turning notifications back on starts with newly detected articles from that point forward.

Scheduling is OFF by default, so toasts can appear at any time while the popup bell is ON. Turn it on to limit notifications to the days and hours you select. Times use your computer’s local time. Items found outside the allowed hours are discarded and are never queued.

Toasts appear one at a time. Choose how many of the newest newly detected posts to queue per updated feed. When several feeds update together, posts are interleaved so each feed gets its newest post first. Posts published while Chrome is closed are skipped when Chrome reopens.

Automatic feed checks are spread across the selected interval, so subscriptions refresh in small groups instead of all at once.
